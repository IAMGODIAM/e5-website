Verbal Opt-In Disclosure Script

Read — or faithfully paraphrase — every item below before asking for consent. Offer to repeat anything or write it down. Never skip an item, never enroll without a clear "yes."

The seven disclosures

  1. Who is texting.
    "I'm [your name], with E5 Enclave Incorporated."
  2. What you will receive.
    "We'd text you replies to your questions, plus occasional program or event updates about the topic you asked about — no more than 4 a month."
  3. How often.
    "Message frequency varies — mostly it depends on our conversation."
  4. Cost.
    "Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan."
  5. How to opt out.
    "You can stop our texts any time — just reply STOP to any message."
  6. How to get help.
    "Reply HELP any time and we'll text you our contact information."
  7. Privacy.
    "Our privacy policy is at e5enclave.com/privacy.html — your number is never sold or shared for marketing."
Then ask, reading the number back:
"May I enroll [number] to receive texts from E5 Enclave?"

Yes → log the opt-in below and make sure they receive the confirmation text. No → thank them; do not enroll, do not text.

Verbal opt-in log — keep every record

We write down every verbal opt-in — the date and time, the number, the disclosures we gave, and the team member who recorded it — and we keep that record.
