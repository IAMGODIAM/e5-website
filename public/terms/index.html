Last updated: September 15, 2026

E5 Enclave Incorporated ("E5 Enclave", "we", "us") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. These terms govern our text-messaging (SMS/MMS) program — the messages you may receive from us after you opt in. Our full Terms of Service for e5enclave.com and our Privacy Policy also apply.

1. The program

When you opt in, you agree to receive text messages from E5 Enclave. Message types include:

  • Automated acknowledgments when you text us;
  • Replies from our team to your questions and requests;
  • Occasional program or event updates related to the work you asked about.

Every message identifies E5 Enclave as the sender.

2. How you opt in

You opt in through one of these methods — and only these methods:

  • Texting us: you initiate contact by texting a mobile number we publish on e5enclave.com or in our printed and digital materials, where we state that texting that number enrolls you to receive texts;
  • Verbal consent: you give your mobile number to an E5 Enclave team member and agree to receive texts, after we tell you who will be texting, what messages you will receive and how often, and how to opt out;
  • Written consent: you provide your mobile number in writing with the same disclosures and agree to receive texts from E5 Enclave.

We never obtain consent through a third party, and we never purchase phone numbers. By opting in, you confirm that you are the person authorized to consent for that mobile number.

3. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies depending on the conversation — there is no fixed schedule. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Check with your carrier if you have questions about your plan.

4. Opting out

You can stop receiving messages at any time. Reply STOP (or OPTOUT, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, or STOPALL) to any message. You will receive one final confirmation, and then no further messages unless you re-subscribe. To re-subscribe, reply START.

5. Help

Reply HELP (or INFO) to any message for help. You can also email accounts@e5enclave.com or call (305) 967-0200.
