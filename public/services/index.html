The problem

The second ticket.

The second ticket is the one their first visit should have prevented. The marketplace sent a different contractor to every store — half of them had never seen your register model. The POS vendor was strong on software and gone after go-live, and wouldn't touch the cabling or the back-office network.

Then the line got cut on a Saturday night, and the carrier's backup arrived in a box with a quick-start guide. It's still in the box.

The inversion

One crew.

The nationals send a tech. We send a crew.

One accountable crew on your program — our techs and our field partners, under one named CTO. One throat to choke.

Line 01

POS Systems Contracts

The Saturday night.

Saturday night, the line is out the door — and the register is down. The marketplace tech who answered the ticket had never seen your register model.

We do point-of-sale the way a crew does: new deployments, replacements, and ongoing service contracts — your model, checked for compatibility. We take POS service requests in every state.

For: retailers, restaurants, hospitality groups, and multi-location businesses.

Line 02

Upgrades

The fleet, refreshed.

The auditors have started asking questions, and half your machines can't take the new OS. We plan the refresh around the business day and stage it seat by seat — hardware and software upgrades across your locations, verified as we go.

Your team never sits at a dead desk.

For: businesses outgrowing aging workstations, servers, or network gear.

Line 03

Deployments

Two halves.

The imaging happens before the truck. Machines arrive from the depot imaged and configured, and we put hands on every desk — new systems deployed on schedule, site by site.

For: companies standing up new locations or refreshing their fleet.

Line 04

Rollouts

None left behind.

One change, everywhere, on a date that holds. Pilots first, then regional waves, cut over in sequence — coordinated multi-site rollouts with a single point of accountability, and a serialized close-out at every site.

For: chains and franchises pushing one change everywhere at once.

Line 05

Migrations

Monday morning.

Two tenants, one file share, and a mail server old enough to vote — with a business that can't stop for a migration. We plan it, stage it, and verify it around the business day: email, servers, cloud, and line-of-business apps.

Monday works the way Friday did.

For: businesses changing platforms and needing it to go right the first time.

Line 06

Computer Moves & Relocation

One owner.

An office move splits IT across four vendors — and the accountability gaps land on the project lead. We're one owner for the move timeline and the IT readiness: equipment moved and reconnected, your team back online fast.

Your team walks in Monday and it works the same as Friday.

For: offices moving across the street or across the country.

Line 07

Backup ISP Solutions

Two paths.

Your registers die when the carrier dies. We install a second path — on a different carrier, because your "backup" shouldn't ride the same fiber. Secondary internet that keeps you taking payments when your primary line goes down.

For: companies that lose money the minute the internet drops.
