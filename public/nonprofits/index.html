The problem

The Saturday the database went dark.

Saturday, the fundraiser — and the donor database won't load. Monday, the clinic — and the check-in tablets freeze mid-line. Wednesday, the pantry — and the Wi-Fi dies in the middle of intake.

Your volunteers didn't sign up to fight the printer. Every hour they spend wrestling the tech is an hour the mission doesn't get — the grant unwritten, the family unserved, the bed unfilled.

The crews that serve the commons answer like it matters. Because it does.

The difference

The marketplace wasn't built for you.

Vendors price for margin. We price for the mission.

The marketplace optimizes for margin: whoever is free takes the ticket, nobody learns your mission, and the quote is priced for an enterprise that isn't you. A volunteer hero keeps it alive until they burn out or move on.

One accountable crew on your program — our techs and our field partners, under one named CTO. The same crew the commercial practice runs — pointed at the commons, at mission pricing.

What we won't sell you.

If the donated catalog covers it — donated software, discounted services, the training courses — we'll point you there first. We sell no licenses and take no commissions, so the advice doesn't change with the invoice.

And the honest limits: we're a crew, not a call center. We answer within one business day, and the person who answers knows your name. If your need is outside the seven lines below, we'll tell you that too — before you spend a minute with us.

Seven lines. One handoff.

The same seven lines the crew runs everywhere — reframed for the mission. Every one of them ends the same way: your people trained, your runbook written, the keys in your hands. Capacity, not dependency.
